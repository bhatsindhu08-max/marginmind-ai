Welcome to MarginMind AI. 

MarginMind AI is purpose-built for the single business owner and principal manufacturing operator. Unlike traditional ERPs that only tell you historical accounting numbers from last month, MarginMind AI is a predictive, real-time operating system that answers the core questions deciding your plant's profitability:

1. "If I accept this customer purchase order, will I make profit or burn cash?"
2. "How much money do I actually have to spend on Raw Materials, Direct Labour, Factory Rent, Power/Water, and Production Supplies?"
3. "Do I have enough cash runway to complete this job without taking expensive debt?"
4. "Should I keep this order in the long run if raw material prices rise, or should I drop/renegotiate it?"
5. "What exact selling price should I quote to guarantee my 25% margin?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. APPLICATION ARCHITECTURE: THE 4 CORE WORKSPACES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MarginMind AI is structured into exactly 4 clean, dedicated workspaces in the left navigation sidebar:

  [1. Overview]                    --> Your daily morning health cockpit & hero order viability evaluator.
  [2. Intelligence & Simulation]   --> All predictive visuals: order gatekeeper, live margin surveillance, what-if macro shocks, pricing elasticity, cost forecasts, and market feeds.
  [3. Business Data]               --> Your plant's master data: products catalog, BOM recipes, raw materials inventory, suppliers, document OCR, and export reports.
  [4. Settings]                    --> Factory fixed overheads (monthly rent, power, water), hourly labour wages, and industry profile presets.

Every action in MarginMind AI is direct and 1-click: no artificial approval bureaucracy or multi-step queues. When you decide to adjust a price or book an order, it updates your live catalog immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STEP-BY-STEP SETUP & ONBOARDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Step 2.1: Plant Fixed Overhead & Industry Configuration
Before evaluating orders, input your real plant operational costs:
1. Click [Settings] in the sidebar (or press ⌘K → Settings).
2. Go to "Company & Industry" tab:
   - Select your manufacturing vertical:
     * Metal & Steel Fabrication (e.g. Forged Flanges, Shafts, Brackets)
     * Textile & Garment Manufacturing (e.g. Cotton Twill Shirts, Denim, Knits)
     * Electronics Assembly / SMT (e.g. Industrial Controller PCBAs, Inverters)
     * Food Processing & Packaging (e.g. Refined Edible Oils, Flour, Pouches)
3. Go to "Financial & OPEX" tab:
   - Enter your facility's real monthly fixed overheads:
     * Monthly Factory Rent (₹)
     * Monthly Electricity & Power Bill (₹)
     * Monthly Water Bill (₹)
     * Maintenance & Factory Admin (₹)
     * Standard Hourly Labour Rate (₹/hr)
     * Target Corporate Hurdle Margin (default: 25%)
     * Critical Margin Floor (default: 16%)
   - MarginMind deterministically absorbs these facility fixed costs across your unit production volume so no product or order is ever evaluated with ₹0 overhead!

3. MANAGING YOUR PLANT DATA (BUSINESS DATA WORKSPACE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The [Business Data] workspace stores your company's operational records:

### 3.1 Products Catalog ([Business Data] -> Products)
- View your finished goods, current selling prices, unit variable costs, and realized margins.
- Click "+ Add Product":
  * Enter Product Name and SKU.
  * Enter Target Selling Price (₹).
  * Enter Primary Raw Material Name (e.g. Steel Scrap / Cotton Yarn) and Unit Material Cost (₹).
  * Enter Direct Labour Cost (₹) and Fixed Overhead Allocation (₹).
  * Result: The product is created with accurate cost economics from day one—never ₹0!
- Delete unwanted or test products anytime with the 1-click trash icon.

### 3.2 Raw Materials & In-Hand Inventory ([Business Data] -> Materials & BOM)
- View your raw materials list, current unit cost, stock in hand, and total in-stock inventory valuation.
- Click "+ Add Raw Material":
  * Enter Material Name and Category.
  * Enter Current In-Stock Quantity (e.g. 5,000 kg).
  * Enter Purchase Unit Rate (₹/kg or ₹/meter) and Minimum Reorder Level.
- Delete or update materials with 1 click.

### 3.3 Sourcing Partners & Vendor Quotations ([Business Data] -> Suppliers)
- Maintain your supplier directory and active price quotes.
- Click "+ Add Sourcing Partner":
  * Enter Vendor Name and Contact.
  * Enter Material Supplied (e.g. Aluminium Billet 6061-T6).
  * Enter Quoted Rate / Price (₹) and Unit.
  * Enter Delivery Lead Time (days) and Reliability Rating.
  * Result: MarginMind saves the supplier and immediately records an active quotation for your BOM calculations.

### 3.4 Document OCR & Automated Extraction ([Business Data] -> Documents)
- Drag and drop customer purchase orders, vendor invoices, quotation PDFs, or CSV/Excel files.
- MarginMind AI extracts:
  * Customer / Vendor Name
  * Document / Order Reference Number
  * Product / Material Description
  * Quantity & Unit Price
  * Delivery Horizon (Days)
- Review the extracted fields in the live editable review form.
- Direct 1-Click Action Buttons:
  * [Book as Customer Order]: Immediately enters the contract into your Order Book.
  * [Save to Products]: Adds the extracted item to your master products catalog.
  * [Save Supplier Quotation]: Registers the vendor's price into your quotation history.

4. THE DEEP ORDER VIABILITY EVALUATOR (CORE DECISION ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Located prominently at the top of the [Overview] dashboard and inside [Intelligence & Simulation -> Order Feasibility]:

Whenever a customer sends an RFQ or purchase order, enter the Product Name, Quantity, and Quoted Unit Price (or select an uploaded document). MarginMind instantly answers 3 critical questions:

### Question 1: Is It Profitable?
- Shows Total Order Revenue, Total Production Cost, and Net Profit.
- Shows Realized Gross Margin % compared against your 25% hurdle and 16% critical floor.
- Clear status badge: PROFITABLE (Green) or LOSS-MAKING (Red).

### Question 2: Money I Have vs. Money I Must Spend
- Detailed spend decomposition across the 5 pillars of manufacturing:
  1. Raw Material Spend (₹): Exact material required based on BOM recipes.
  2. Direct Labour Spend (₹): Line worker wages and crew hours.
  3. Factory Rent & Facilities (₹): Plant overhead allocation for the job.
  4. Production Supply & Utilities (₹): Electricity, water, tooling consumables.
  5. Time Required: Production days needed to complete the volume.
- Cash Runway Health Check:
  * Compares your available working capital against total cash outflow required.
  * Tells you whether you can self-fund the job or risk cash-flow starvation before customer payment arrives.

### Question 3: Future Sensitivity & Keep/Drop Advisory
- What if raw material prices surge +10% to +18% on commodity markets?
- The stress engine calculates your "Stressed Margin %" under inflation.
- Actionable Strategic Advisory:
  * KEEP_ORDER: Margin remains safe and resilient. Accept and book immediately.
  * RENEGOTIATE_PRICE: Material inflation compresses margin below target. Click [Apply Profitable Price (₹...)] to instantly set the suggested price and re-test.
  * KEEP_BUT_LOCK_SUPPLIER: Profitable now, but vulnerable to spot material spikes. Immediately lock fixed-price supplier contracts.
  * DROP_ORDER: Negative contribution margin. Order burns cash. Reject or renegotiate before signing.

5. INTELLIGENCE & SIMULATION WORKSPACE (THE VISUAL ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All predictive tools and visual simulations live together in [Intelligence & Simulation]:

### Tab 1: Order Feasibility (OrderSure™)
- The gatekeeper for your order book.
- Table of all open customer contracts, agreed unit prices, current margins, stress margins, and keep/drop verdicts.
- Delete completed or canceled orders with the 1-click trash button.

### Tab 2: Margin Surveillance (MarginGuard™)
- Visual charts tracking product margins across your entire portfolio.
- Real-time margin variance waterfall: breaks down whether margin erosion is caused by Raw Materials, Labour, or Utilities.
- Identifies which products breach the 16% critical floor and calculates the exact price recovery needed.

### Tab 3: What-If Macro Economic Shocks
- Select an external commodity benchmark (e.g. LME Aluminium, MCX Cotton, WPI Steel) and simulate an economic price shock (+5% to +35%).
- The Knowledge Graph propagates the cost spike through your raw materials, recalculates every BOM, and shows you:
  * Total Monthly Cost Increase across your factory.
  * Number of products pushed into the loss-making zone.
  * Recommended selling price revisions for affected items.

### Tab 4: Pricing Simulator (Elasticity Engine)
- Interactive price adjustment slider (-30% to +50%).
- Microeconomic demand elasticity modeling: shows how raising or lowering price affects customer order volume, unit margins, and total annual plant profit.

### Tab 5: Multi-Horizon Cost Forecasting
- Predictive econometric projections across 4 business horizons:
  * 0–3 Months (Tactical spot buying)
  * 3–12 Months (Annual contract pricing)
  * 1–3 Years (Tooling & equipment lifecycle)
  * 3–5 Years (Long-term product portfolio viability)
- Outputs Favourable, Base, and Stress scenario fans.

### Tab 6: Supply Risk Radar (SupplyShock™)
- Identifies materials dependent on a single supplier.
- Computes Days-of-Supply inventory buffers based on open orders (alerts when buffer is <15 days).
- Lists pre-qualified alternate suppliers with quoted prices and lead times.

### Tab 7: Market Signals & Commodity Indices
- Real-time feeds from official economic benchmarks:
  * MCX (Multi Commodity Exchange of India)
  * LME (London Metal Exchange)
  * World Bank Commodity Pink Sheet
  * MoCI Wholesale Price Index (WPI)
  * RBI Foreign Exchange Reference Rates (USD/INR, EUR/INR)

### Tab 8: Break-Even Economics
- Fixed vs. Variable cost decomposition for every SKU.
- Calculates exact monthly break-even unit volumes and unit contribution margins (Selling Price - Variable Costs).
